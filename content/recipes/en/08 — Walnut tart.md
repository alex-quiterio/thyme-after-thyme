# Walnut tart

## Pastry

  - 150 g margarine
  - 250 g plain flour
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp vinegar
  - 1 egg
  - 1 pinch of salt

## Filling

  - 200 g sugar
  - 3 eggs
  - 100 g margarine
  - 1 cup chopped walnuts
  - 1 cup chopped almonds
  - 1 cup raisins
  - 1 pinch of salt

## Method

Combine the margarine, sugar, vinegar and egg. Add the flour a little at a time and mix until even.

Line a tart tin with the dough, pressing it in well.

Separate the eggs. Beat the yolks with the sugar and margarine.

Beat the whites with a pinch of salt until firm. Fold them gently into the yolk mixture.

Fold in the walnuts, almonds and raisins.

Preheat the oven to 170 °C. Pre-bake the pastry until it starts to set.

Add the filling and bake for about 25 minutes, until golden and slightly crisp.

Let it cool before slicing.
