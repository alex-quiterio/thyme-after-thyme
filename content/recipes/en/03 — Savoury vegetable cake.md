# Savoury vegetable cake

## Base

  - 200 g plain flour
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - 1/4 tsp pepper
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - 3 large eggs
  - 100 ml milk
  - 100 ml vegetable oil

## Filling

Choose 2 to 4:

  - courgette
  - roasted pepper
  - mushrooms
  - spinach
  - leek
  - olives
  - sun-dried tomato
  - feta
  - grated cheese
  - fresh herbs

## Method

Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and pepper.

Add the eggs and fold in. Add the milk, then the oil. Switch to a spatula as the batter thickens.

Fold in the vegetables and the remaining fillings.

Pour into a lined loaf tin or cake tin.

Bake for about 50 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out almost clean.

Rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.

## The best version

Roast the mushrooms and courgette before adding them. They lose water in the oven and stop the cake turning soggy.
