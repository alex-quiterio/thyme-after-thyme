# Sunday vegetable roast

*This is less a recipe and more a ritual.*

## Choose

  - potatoes
  - carrots
  - parsnip
  - pumpkin
  - cauliflower
  - onions
  - garlic
  - mushrooms

## Season

  - olive oil
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - rosemary or thyme
  - smoked paprika
  - lemon

## Method

Heat the oven to 220 °C.

Cut everything into large pieces.

Put the potatoes and the hardest vegetables on the tray first.

Season generously and roast for 20 minutes.

Add the more tender vegetables and the herbs.

Keep roasting for 25–35 minutes.

Add the garlic for the last 10–15 minutes.

Finish everything with lemon juice.

Serve with béchamel, gravy, hummus, tahini, fried eggs, beans or rustic bread.

The leftovers become tomorrow's lunch.
