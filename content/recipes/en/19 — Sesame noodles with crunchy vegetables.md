# Sesame noodles with crunchy vegetables

## Ingredients

  - 250 g noodles
  - 1 carrot
  - 1/2 cucumber
  - 1 pepper
  - 2 spring onions
  - sesame seeds

## Sauce

  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - 1 tbsp peanut butter or tahini
  - 1 tbsp lime juice
  - 1 tsp brown sugar
  - 1 clove garlic
  - fresh ginger
  - chilli (optional)
  - 2–3 tbsp water

## Method

Cook the noodles and rinse them quickly in cold water.

Cut the carrot, cucumber and pepper into matchsticks.

Whisk the sauce ingredients.

Toss the noodles with the sauce and vegetables.

Finish with spring onion, sesame and lime.

For a complete meal, add crispy tofu, edamame, mushrooms or toasted peanuts.
