# Tiramisù

## Ingredients

  - 3 whole eggs
  - 1 extra yolk
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 500 g mascarpone
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - 1 packet of pavesini or ladyfingers
  - strong coffee
  - unsweetened cocoa powder

## Method

Separate the eggs.

Beat the yolks with the sugar until pale and creamy. Add the mascarpone.

Beat the whites with the salt until firm.

Gently fold the whites into the mascarpone mixture.

Dip the biscuits quickly in cold coffee.

Layer biscuits and mascarpone cream.

Finish with cocoa.

Refrigerate for at least 2–3 hours, ideally overnight.
