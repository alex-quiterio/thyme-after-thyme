# Spinach, feta and herb pie

## Ingredients

  - 500 g spinach
  - 200 g feta
  - 1 onion
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - fresh dill, parsley or mint
  - 2 eggs
  - olive oil
  - filo pastry
  - black pepper
  - 1 pinch of nutmeg

## Method

Soften the onion in olive oil until tender.

Add the garlic and spinach. Cook until wilted.

Let cool a little and squeeze out the excess liquid.

Mix with the feta, eggs, herbs, pepper and nutmeg.

Lay sheets of filo in a tray, brushing each with olive oil.

Add the filling. Cover with more filo and brush the top with olive oil.

Bake at 180–190 °C for 35–45 minutes, until deeply golden.

Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.
