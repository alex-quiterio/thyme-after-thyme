# Caramelised onion and mushroom toast

## Ingredients

  - 2 onions
  - 250 g mushrooms
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - olive oil
  - butter (optional)
  - 1 tsp thyme
  - 1 tsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp vinegar
  - sourdough bread
  - cream cheese or hummus (optional)
  - black pepper

## Method

Slice the onions thinly.

Cook slowly in olive oil with salt, 20–30 minutes, until deeply golden.

Add the mushrooms and turn up the heat. Cook until golden.

Add the garlic and thyme.

Finish with the soy sauce and vinegar.

Toast the bread. Spread with hummus or cream cheese, if using.

Top with the mushrooms and onions.

Finish with black pepper and herbs.
