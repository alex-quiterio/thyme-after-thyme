# Vegetable béchamel

## Ingredients

  - 1 litre milk or unsweetened plant-based drink
  - 100 g butter or margarine
  - 100 g flour
  - salt
  - freshly grated nutmeg
  - black pepper (optional)

## Method

Heat the milk.

Melt the butter in another pot. Add the flour a little at a time, stirring constantly. Cook for 1–2 minutes without browning.

Add the hot milk little by little, whisking continuously.

Once combined, stir for 5–10 minutes, until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.

Season with salt and nutmeg.

## Uses

Lasagne, vegetable gratin, cauliflower cheese, savoury crêpes and baked pasta.
