# Almond tart

## Ingredients

  - 3 eggs
  - 200 g sugar
  - 50 g butter
  - 200 g ground almonds
  - zest of 1 lemon
  - 1 pinch of salt

## Method

Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

Beat the eggs with the sugar until paler and slightly thick.

Melt the butter and let it cool a little.

Fold in the almonds, lemon zest, salt and butter.

Pour into a lined 20 cm tart tin.

Bake for 25–30 minutes, until golden and set.

Let it cool before slicing and dust with icing sugar, if you like.
