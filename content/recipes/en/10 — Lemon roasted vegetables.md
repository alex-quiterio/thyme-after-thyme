# Lemon roasted vegetables

## Ingredients

  - 1 courgette
  - 1 aubergine
  - 2 peppers
  - 1 red onion
  - 3 cloves garlic
  - olive oil
  - 1 lemon
  - smoked paprika
  - cumin
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - fresh herbs

## Method

Heat the oven to 220 °C.

Cut the vegetables into similar-sized pieces.

Toss with olive oil, garlic, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper.

Spread on a large tray; don't overcrowd.

Roast 30–40 minutes, turning once.

Finish with lemon zest and juice and fresh herbs.

Serve with rice, couscous, toasted bread, hummus or yoghurt.
