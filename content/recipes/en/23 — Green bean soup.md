# Green bean soup

## Method 1: Traditional vegetable base

### Ingredients

  - Potatoes
  - Carrots
  - Pumpkin
  - Onion
  - Cloves of garlic
  - Green beans (cut thinly)
  - Carrot (cut into small pieces)
  - Water
  - Salt to taste
  - Olive oil

### Preparation

1.  In a pot (pressure cooker or not), put the water, salt, potatoes, onion, garlic, pumpkin and carrots.
2.  Bring to the boil and cook everything until very soft.
3.  When the vegetables are cooked, blend everything with a stick blender until you have a creamy, even base.
4.  Add the thinly cut green beans and the small pieces of carrot to the base.
5.  Cook a little longer until the green beans and carrot are tender.
6.  Add the olive oil almost at the end of cooking to preserve the flavour and avoid overcooking.

## Method 2: Dried bean base

### Ingredients

  - Dried beans (soaked)
  - Onion
  - Cloves of garlic
  - Pumpkin
  - Carrots
  - Green beans (cut)
  - Carrot (cut into small pieces)
  - Water and salt to taste
  - Olive oil

### Preparation

1.  Put the soaked dried beans in the pot with the onion, garlic, pumpkin and carrots.
2.  Cook until all the ingredients are soft.
3.  Blend the mixture with a stick blender to make the base.
4.  Add the cut green beans and the small pieces of carrot.
5.  Cook until done, and finish with olive oil at the end.
