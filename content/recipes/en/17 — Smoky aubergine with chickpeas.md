# Smoky aubergine with chickpeas

## Ingredients

  - 2 aubergines
  - 1 tin chickpeas
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1/2 tsp cumin
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 lemon
  - 2 tbsp tahini
  - parsley
  - salt

## Method

Cut the aubergines into cubes.

Toss with the olive oil, paprika, cumin and salt.

Roast at 220 °C for about 30 minutes.

Add the chickpeas and roast 10 minutes more.

Mix the tahini with the lemon juice and enough water to make a creamy sauce.

Arrange the aubergine and chickpeas on a plate. Drizzle with the tahini and finish with parsley and lemon zest.
