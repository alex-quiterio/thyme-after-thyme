# Sticky shiitake with ginger and sesame

*Serves 3–4*

## Ingredients

  - 300 g rice
  - 300 g shiitake mushrooms
  - 2 tbsp cornstarch
  - 4 cm fresh ginger
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 3 spring onions
  - 2 tbsp neutral oil or olive oil
  - 1 tbsp brown sugar
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - 60 ml soy sauce
  - 75 ml water
  - sesame seeds, to sprinkle
  - lime juice (optional)

## Method

Cook the rice.

Clean the shiitake and cut them into thick slices. Toss them in the cornstarch until lightly coated.

Slice the ginger and garlic. Slice the spring onions finely, keeping some of the green tops for the end.

Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook 5–7 minutes, until golden and starting to crisp. Remove and set aside.

Lower the heat. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for about 1 minute.

Add the brown sugar and stir until it starts to melt and caramelise.

Add the sesame oil, soy sauce and water. Turn up the heat and let the sauce bubble until glossy.

Return the mushrooms and toss until coated.

Fold in the spring onions. Finish with sesame seeds and lime.

## Variations

Use oyster mushrooms, add broccoli or pak choi, swap the sugar for maple syrup, or add chilli crisp.
