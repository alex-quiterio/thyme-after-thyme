# Green shakshuka

## Ingredients

  - 1 onion
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1 leek
  - 1 courgette
  - 300 g spinach
  - 4–6 eggs
  - olive oil
  - cumin
  - chilli flakes
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - feta (optional)
  - fresh herbs

## Method

Slowly soften the onion and leek in olive oil.

Add the courgette and cook until tender.

Add the garlic, cumin and chilli.

Fold in the spinach.

Make wells in the vegetables and crack an egg into each.

Cover and cook until the whites are set but the yolks are still soft.

Finish with feta, herbs and black pepper.

Serve with good bread.
