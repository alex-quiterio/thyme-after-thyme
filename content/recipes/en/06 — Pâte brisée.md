# Pâte brisée

## Ingredients

  - 250 g plain flour
  - 100 g butter
  - 70 ml cold water
  - 1 pinch of salt

## Method

Mix the flour and salt.

Add the cold butter, rubbing it in with your fingers until it looks like coarse sand.

Add the water a little at a time, until the dough only just comes together.

Shape into a disc and chill for at least 30 minutes.

Roll out and use for tarts or savoury pies.

For a tart, blind-bake the pastry at 180 °C for about 15 minutes before adding the filling.

**Cold butter + minimal handling = tender pastry.**
