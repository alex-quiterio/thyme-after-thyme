# Egg-free apple cake

## Ingredients

  - 3 small apples or 2 large
  - 110 g icing sugar
  - 250 ml milk
  - 70 ml sunflower oil
  - 250 g flour
  - 1 sachet of baking powder

## Preparation

Cut the apples into slices.

In a bowl, combine the dry ingredients.

In another, combine the liquids.

Mix the liquids into the dry ingredients.

Pour a little of the batter into the tin.

Add the rest of the apple, diced, keeping some aside for decoration.

Optional: add walnuts and raisins.

Arrange the apple slices in a fan on top.

Cover with the remaining batter.

Bake at 180 °C for 60 minutes.
