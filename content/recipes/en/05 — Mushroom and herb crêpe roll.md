# Mushroom and herb crêpe roll

## Crêpe batter

  - 1.5 cups flour
  - 1.5 cups plant-based drink
  - 1.5 tbsp melted butter or margarine
  - 5 eggs
  - 1 pinch of salt

## Filling

  - 400 g mushrooms
  - 1 onion
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - fresh parsley or coriander
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - 2–3 tbsp vegetable béchamel
  - grated cheese (optional)

## Method

Mix the milk and flour into a smooth batter. Add the melted butter.

Separate the eggs. Add the yolks to the batter.

Beat the whites to soft peaks and fold them gently into the batter.

Make thin crêpes in a lightly greased non-stick pan.

For the filling, sauté the onion and garlic. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook over high heat until golden.

Season and fold in the herbs and béchamel.

Spread the filling over the crêpes and roll up.

Place in a baking dish, cover with béchamel and cheese if you like, and bake at 180 °C for 15–20 minutes.

Cut on the diagonal and serve.
