# Leek à brás

*A vegetarian version of bacalhau à brás, made with leek, potato, onion and egg. Serves 3–4.*

## Ingredients

  - 1 large packet of potato sticks (straw fries)
  - 1 leek
  - 1 onion
  - 4 eggs
  - 1 handful of olives
  - coriander or fresh parsley
  - olive oil
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - 1 tbsp white wine vinegar (optional)
  - a splash of white wine (optional)

## Method

Slice the onion and leek thinly.

Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. Add the onion, salt and black pepper and cook over low heat until soft.

Add the leek and keep cooking until tender and sweet.

Add the potato sticks and toss them through the vegetables until slightly softened.

Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and, if you like, a splash of white wine.

Lower the heat and pour in the eggs. Stir gently; the aim is creamy egg, not scrambled egg.

Take off the heat while still slightly runny.

Finish with the herbs and olives. A few drops of white wine vinegar give the dish a bright lift.
