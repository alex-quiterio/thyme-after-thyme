# Crispy potatoes with garlic, herbs and vinegar

## Ingredients

  - 1 kg baby potatoes
  - 4 cloves garlic
  - olive oil
  - 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  - parsley or coriander
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - smoked paprika (optional)

## Method

Boil the potatoes in salted water until just tender.

Drain and let them steam dry.

Lightly crush each potato.

Drizzle with olive oil and season generously.

Roast at 220 °C for 30–40 minutes, turning once.

Add the garlic for the last 10 minutes.

Finish with vinegar and herbs.

The vinegar isn't decoration. It cuts through the starch and fat and makes the whole dish brighter.
