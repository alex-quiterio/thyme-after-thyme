# Tomato, white bean and olive stew

## Ingredients

  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 onion
  - 3 cloves garlic
  - 1 red pepper
  - 400 g chopped tomatoes
  - 2 tins white beans
  - 1 handful of olives
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1/2 tsp cumin
  - chilli flakes
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - fresh parsley

## Method

Soften the onion in the olive oil until tender.

Add the pepper and cook for 5 minutes.

Add the garlic, paprika, cumin and chilli. Cook for 30 seconds.

Add the tomatoes and simmer gently for 15 minutes.

Add the beans and olives. Cook 10 minutes more.

Taste and adjust the seasoning.

Finish with parsley and olive oil.

Serve with rustic bread.
