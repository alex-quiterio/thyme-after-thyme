# Chocolate salami

## Ingredients

  - 1 packet of Oro Saiwa biscuits (250 g)
  - 200 g sugar
  - 125 g shelled walnuts
  - 2 eggs
  - 100 g butter
  - 50 g unsweetened cocoa

## Preparation

Mix all the ingredients.

Use baking paper to shape the mixture into a log, like a salami.

Rest in the freezer for 2–3 hours.
