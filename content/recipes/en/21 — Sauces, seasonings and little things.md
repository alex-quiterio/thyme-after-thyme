# Sauces, seasonings and little things

## Lemon tahini

  - 3 tbsp tahini
  - juice of 1 lemon
  - 1 small clove garlic
  - cold water
  - salt

Mix everything, adding water until silky.

## Herb oil

  - 1 big handful of parsley
  - 1 big handful of coriander or basil
  - 100 ml olive oil
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - lemon zest

Blitz or chop finely. Drizzle over roasted vegetables, beans, eggs or bread.

## Quick mustard vinaigrette

  - 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  - 1 tbsp vinegar
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  - salt
  - black pepper

Shake everything in a jar.

## Garlic yoghurt

  - 200 g yoghurt
  - 1 clove garlic
  - lemon juice
  - salt
  - olive oil

Mix and leave to rest for 10 minutes.

## Quick pickled onion

  - 1 red onion
  - 100 ml vinegar
  - 100 ml water
  - 1 tsp sugar
  - 1 tsp salt

Slice the onion thinly.

Heat the vinegar, water, sugar and salt.

Pour over the onion and leave to rest for at least 30 minutes.

Keep in the fridge.
