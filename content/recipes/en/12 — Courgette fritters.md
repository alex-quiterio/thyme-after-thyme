# Courgette fritters

## Ingredients

  - 2 courgettes
  - 2 eggs
  - 3–4 tbsp flour
  - 1 clove garlic
  - 1 handful of fresh herbs
  - 50 g feta (optional)
  - salt
  - black pepper
  - olive oil

## Method

Grate the courgettes, salt lightly and leave to rest for 10 minutes.

Squeeze out as much liquid as you can.

Mix with the eggs, flour, garlic, herbs, pepper and feta.

Fry spoonfuls in a thin layer of olive oil, 2–3 minutes on each side.

Serve with yoghurt, lemon and herbs.
