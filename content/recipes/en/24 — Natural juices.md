**Yellow Juice**

  - 1 small collard green leaf (no stalks)
  - 1/3 cucumber
  - 1 slice of pineapple
  - turmeric (1 teaspoon)
  - 1 teaspoon ground flaxseed
  - water or ginger tea

**Orange Juice**

  - 1 cooked carrot
  - juice of 1 orange
  - ginger juice (1 tablespoon)
  - chia seeds (1 dessertspoon)
  - water or cinnamon tea

**White Juice**

  - 2 celery stalks, peeled
  - ginger juice (1 dessertspoon)
  - ground flaxseed (1 teaspoon)
  - 1 apple
  - water or white tea

**Green Juice 1**

  - 1 leaf of kale or spinach (a handful)
  - 2 mint leaves
  - 1 apple
  - juice of ½ lemon
  - 1 teaspoon ginger juice (grate and squeeze; discard the fibres)
  - water or green tea

**Green Juice 2**

  - 1 celery stalk (peeled) or spinach (a handful)
  - 1/3 avocado
  - 1 kiwi
  - juice of ½ lemon
  - water or green tea

**Red Juice**

  - ½ cucumber
  - 80 g blueberries
  - 1/3 cooked beetroot
  - lemon juice (1 tablespoon)
  - 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  - water or rooibos tea
