# Walnut tart (baking-powder version)

## Pastry

  - 150 g margarine
  - 250 g plain flour (not self-raising)
  - 1 tablespoon sugar
  - 1 tablespoon vinegar
  - 1 teaspoon baking powder
  - 1 egg

## Filling

  - 200 g sugar
  - 3 eggs
  - 100 g margarine
  - 1 full cup of: chopped walnuts, sultanas and chopped almonds

## Preparation: pastry

For the pastry, combine the sugar and vinegar with the margarine and egg and mix well until smooth.

Add the flour gradually and mix until combined, with no lumps.

## Preparation: filling

For the filling, separate the 3 eggs into yolks and whites. Add the yolks to the sugar and margarine.

Beat the whites to soft peaks, adding a pinch of salt as you go.

Fold in the whites gradually.

Finally, add the sultanas, walnuts and almonds.

## Tip

While you make the filling, put the pastry in an oven preheated to about 170 °C and let it bake lightly.

When the pastry starts to turn crisper, add the rest of the filling and bake for a further 25 minutes.
