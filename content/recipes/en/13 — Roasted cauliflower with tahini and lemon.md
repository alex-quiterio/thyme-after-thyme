# Roasted cauliflower with tahini and lemon

## Ingredients

  - 1 cauliflower
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - salt
  - black pepper

## Tahini sauce

  - 4 tbsp tahini
  - juice of 1 lemon
  - 1 clove garlic
  - cold water
  - salt

## To finish

  - parsley
  - toasted sesame seeds
  - chilli flakes

## Method

Heat the oven to 220 °C.

Break the cauliflower into florets. Toss with the olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.

Roast 30–35 minutes, until golden.

Whisk the tahini with the lemon. When it thickens, add cold water a little at a time until creamy. Add the garlic and salt.

Drizzle over the cauliflower and finish with parsley, sesame and chilli.
