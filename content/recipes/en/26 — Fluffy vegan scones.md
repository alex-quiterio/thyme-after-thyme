# Fluffy vegan scones

*Makes 8–10*

## Dry ingredients

  - 2 cups (about 250 g) flour
  - 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  - ¼ teaspoon baking soda
  - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - 3 tablespoons sugar (I use icing sugar)

## Fat

  - ⅓ cup (about 75 g) cold vegan margarine or butter

## Liquid

  - ¾ cup (about 180 ml) plant-based milk
  - 1 teaspoon lemon juice or apple cider vinegar
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla (optional)

## Method

Vegan buttermilk: mix the milk with the lemon juice and leave aside for 5 minutes.

Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl: flour, baking powder, salt, sugar and baking soda.

Cut the margarine into small cubes and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs.

Add the liquids and mix gently with a spoon. Don't knead: a shaggy, slightly sticky dough is perfect.

Turn onto a floured surface and pat (don't roll) into a circle 2.5 cm thick. Use a small glass or mug to cut out rounds. The thicker, the taller and fluffier the scone.

Bake at 220 °C for 12–15 minutes (18–20 minutes in our oven), until tall and lightly browned. Optional: brush the tops with milk first.
