# Oyster Mushroom and White Wine Braise

A one-pan vegetarian braise for two, about 20 minutes.

## Gather

  - A couple of potatoes
  - 1 leek
  - 1 onion
  - 250 g oyster mushrooms
  - Cooked beans, drained
  - Cooked lentils, a handful
  - Fresh coriander
  - A good splash of white wine
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper
  - Pickles and beetroot, to serve on the side

## Prep

  - Dice the potatoes small, roughly 1 cm cubes, so they fry through fast.
  - Slice the leek and onion.
  - Tear the oyster mushrooms into strips by hand, don't dice them.
  - Drain the beans and lentils. Chop the coriander.

## Cook

1.  Heat oil in a wide pan. Add the diced potato first with salt and pepper. Fry until the edges start to go golden.
2.  Add the leek and onion. Cook until soft and colouring.
3.  Add the oyster mushrooms, giving them space so they sear rather than steam. Do two batches if the pan is crowded. Cook a few minutes until golden.
4.  Pour in a good splash of white wine. Let it bubble away for a minute or two.
5.  Tip in the beans and lentils, just to warm through.
6.  Kill the heat. Throw the chopped coriander over the top.
7.  Serve with pickles and raw beetroot on the side for sharp contrast.
